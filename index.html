What I built

1
Instrument

XLSForm questionnaire with skip logic, constraints and GPS capture, deployed on KoboToolbox.

2
Live monitoring

A GitHub Pages dashboard fed by the Kobo API through scheduled GitHub Actions, refreshing every hour for supervisors.

3
Analysis pipeline

Reproducible SPSS syntax that recodes exports and derives FCS, HFIAS, rCSI, MDD-W and Washington Group disability flags.

4
Quality catch

Spotted Excel silently stripping underscores from income bracket codes and fixed the recode before any table was produced.

Factors Influencing Knowledge Sharing Behaviour of Police Officers in Ibadan Metropolis, Nigeria

Adekannbi, J. O. & Bello, O. (2021). Journal of Information & Knowledge Management, 20(2), 1–23. World Scientific.

  • Built from my MSc Information Science thesis at the University of Ibadan, co-authored with my supervisor, Dr Janet Adekannbi
  • Surveyed 134 officers at Oyo State Police Headquarters with a structured questionnaire, using a modified Theory of Reasoned Action
  • Found that leadership, trust, rewards, reciprocal benefits and enjoyment in helping others shaped officers' intention to share knowledge
